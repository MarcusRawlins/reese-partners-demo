The problem

Your business grew. Your tech just piled up.

Most established companies didn't choose their stack. It accumulated: one tool per problem, one login per person, and nobody whose job it is to make it all work together.

Tool sprawl

Subscriptions nobody remembers buying, overlapping apps and seats you pay for but don't use.

Manual busywork

Your team copies data between systems, chases follow-ups and rebuilds the same reports every week.

AI on the back burner

You know AI could help. Nobody has the time to figure out where, set it up safely and keep it running.

No one owns it

Freelancers, agencies and that one employee who's "good with computers." When something breaks, everyone points somewhere else.
